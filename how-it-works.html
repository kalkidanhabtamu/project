HOW IT WORKS

From gesture to speech.

Handspeak creates a simple bridge between hand gestures and spoken communication.

01
CAMERA

Show your gesture

Position your hands in front of your device camera. Handspeak uses the camera to observe your hand movements.

02
DETECT

Hand detection

The recognition system analyzes hand position, movement and gesture patterns to identify the intended sign.

03
TEXT

Translation

Recognized gestures are converted into words and combined into understandable sentences.

04
VOICE

Speech output

Finally, the translated sentence can be converted into speech so that others can hear the message.

DESIGNED FOR ACCESSIBILITY

Communication should be accessible to everyone.
