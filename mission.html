OUR MISSION

Giving every gesture a voice.

Handspeak was created around a simple idea: communication should never be limited by the way someone communicates.

We are building technology that helps connect sign language users and speaking communities through accessible gesture translation.

H

Communication for everyone.

Technology should bring people closer, not create new barriers.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Three principles guide Handspeak.

01

Accessibility

We believe digital tools should be usable by people with different communication needs.

02

Connection

Our technology aims to make conversations between signing and speaking communities easier.

03

Innovation

We continuously explore new technology to improve gesture recognition and translation.
